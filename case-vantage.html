01About the project

Vantage moved to agents.
The brand stayed behind.

Vantage launched as a fast network for real-time apps. Then the company shifted its focus to agents: programs that read live data, run models and act on-chain in real time. The product moved on. The brand didn’t. It still spoke to an older audience, blended in with every other network and no longer represented the company.

02The challenge

Make agents visible
at a glance.

Networks tend to look alike. Gradients, glossy coins, a stylised letter. Vantage needed a look that read as autonomous agents at first sight, with one mark that could stretch across nine products without losing its shape.

  • AOutgrown, not broken

    The company had grown. The brand still described the old one.

  • BA template look

    No shape, no personality, nothing that said who they are.

  • CA crowded field

    Every competitor leans the same way. Standing apart was the brief.

  • DExperts judging

    Their audience spots lazy design in seconds.

03Exploration

Three rounds, one route.

We explored three directions in parallel: a geometric system, a type-led mark and a signal pattern. The type-led route won because it scaled from a favicon to a building wrap without redrawing.

04The mark

Brackets that hold
the agent.

Two brackets wrap a single glyph: the agent sits inside the system, the system frames the agent. It reads in code, on stage and on a phone.

05Sub-brands

Nine products,
one family.

Every product gets its own glyph inside the same brackets, its own accent inside a shared palette, and a naming rule that keeps the family obvious.

Built to be trusted from the first click

Complex products live or die on clarity. We turn dense ideas into interfaces and brands people understand at a glance.

Make the future make sense to everyone

Big ideas don’t sell themselves. We shape them into brands people want to use.

Design for what’s next, not what’s now

We partner with ambitious teams building ahead of the curve, and ship the brands that help them get there first.
