Privacy Policy

01

Privacy Policy

At datum.studio we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it and how it is used.

The short version: this site has no accounts and no advertising trackers. What we hold is what you choose to send us, plus anonymous statistics about how the site is used.

02

Who is responsible for your data?

This website is operated by:

Datum Studio Lda
Porto, Portugal
Contact: Elias Marin
Email: hello@datum.studio

Datum Studio Lda is the data controller for the information described on this page.

03

What information we collect

Messages you send us. When you use the contact form we receive your email address, the service you picked, your budget if you shared one, and your message.

Bookings. When you book an intro call we receive your name, email and the time you chose.

Anonymous analytics. Page views and referrers, aggregated, with no cookies and no personal identifiers.

04

How we use your information

We use your information for the reason you gave it to us, and for nothing unrelated.

  • To reply and prepare a proposal. We act on your request before any contract exists.
  • To deliver the work. Running the project, keeping you updated and invoicing as part of our contract with you.
  • To meet legal obligations. We keep invoices and accounting records because tax law requires it.
  • To run and protect the site. We rely on our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used and in keeping spam out.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or make automated decisions about you.

05

Cookies and analytics

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies. Our analytics work without cookies and do not follow you across sites.

One preference is saved in your browser’s local storage: whether you have turned the site’s sound off. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.

06

Who we share it with

Only the service providers needed to run the studio: email, hosting, scheduling and accounting. Each one processes data on our behalf under a written agreement.

07

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don’t become projects are deleted after 12 months.
  • Client and project records are kept for the length of our work together and, after that, as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Analytics are kept only in an aggregated form that does not identify you.

You can ask us to delete your information sooner at any time. We will, unless the law requires us to keep it.

08

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct anything inaccurate or incomplete;
  • delete your data;
  • restrict or stop a particular use of it;
  • send your data to you, or to another provider, in a portable format.

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. To use any of these rights, email hello@datum.studio. We reply within one month.

If you believe we have handled your data wrongly you can complain to the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD) or the authority where you live. We’d appreciate the chance to put it right first.

09

How we protect it

We keep the data we hold to a minimum, store it with reputable providers using encryption in transit and at rest, and limit access to the people who need it to do the work.

10

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will be highlighted here for at least 30 days.
